# RainonUI — Changelog

## 1.8.1

* Choose Normal or Gold quality for each eligible reagent in the Professions tab. Craft buttons use the chosen quality and are disabled if any required reagent is missing. Bouquet of Herbs now uses individual selectors only.
* Reagent and crafted-item icons are shown alongside names. The quality menu shows aligned labels next to the quality icons.
* Added Wondrous Synergist. Its craft button uses available charges and selected reagents without spending Concentration.
* The Professions tab can show or hide the Concentration Crafting window. The window opens in the center until moved and has a position lock and a shortcut to Professions settings.
