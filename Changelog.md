# RainonUI — Changelog

## 1.7.1

* **"Macros" button is back in the Order Queue window.** It sits under "Blacklist" (on the Log tab it turns into "Clear") and creates or updates three macros for your action bar: open a Consortium payout bag, open a bouquet of herbs, and press "Next" in the queue.
* **Craft profit in the Procs list now uses the real reagent cost.** Every reagent placed in the recipe window is counted at the moment you press Create, minus what Resourcefulness gives back — so the gold earned per craft is accurate for every recipe.
* **Reworked automatic Start / Complete for patron orders.** The Blizzard order window buttons are pressed for you in a simpler, more reliable way; the queue's "Next" button, keybind and macro work exactly as before.
* Updated credits.
