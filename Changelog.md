# RainonUI — Changelog

## 1.6.0

* **Profession Items panel.** A compact panel on the crafting tab of any profession: Haranir Phial of Ingenuity and Shattered Essence (enchanting) glow when their buff is missing and are used with a click, followed by the Artisan's Consortium star and the three crafting stat boosters with how many you own (bags, bank and warband bank). Drag it anywhere; it hides on the other profession tabs and can be locked in Professions settings. The old separate phial/essence icons are gone, so there is nothing to place in Edit Mode anymore.
* **Knowledge & Charges window improvements.** A new "i" help button explains every column. The Characters menu lets you choose which professions to track per character and whether to track Abundance, and it now scrolls when you have many characters. A new "Spheres" column lets you tick the professions whose knowledge spheres you still need — the order queue then counts sphere rewards as value. Enchanting now tracks its weekly trainer quest correctly and shows the weekly disenchant loot on hover.
* **Professions settings.** New options for the Profession Items panel, locking it in place, and automatically switching between the order queue and the Knowledge window when you change profession tabs.
* **Crafting Order Queue (test module, off by default).** A queue next to the profession window for patron orders with a single "Next" button: claim, craft with the cheapest reagents that reach the required quality, and deliver. It respects your talents, shows expected profit, can place finishing reagents, supports recipes that need a special currency, keeps a blacklist and a watch list for multicraft procs, and builds an Auctionator shopping list for missing reagents. Enable it on the Test tab.
* **Design preview (test).** A preview of the upcoming settings window layout: /rsdes.
* **Fixed** an error on login caused by a missing Break timer sound file. The Break sticker still works; its sound is temporarily muted.
